The pictured effect is LED-based, not a real open flame. Confirm the exact mist mechanism, electrical safety certifications, and operating instructions for the supplier's specific model before use. Keep water and electrical components handled as instructed.

Can I use essential oils?

Only add essential oils if the manufacturer explicitly confirms the tank is oil-compatible. Some diffusers can be damaged by oils, so check the included instructions before adding anything other than water.
