THE PRACTICAL DETAILS

Shipping &
returns.

Clear expectations matter. Final terms should be published here before orders are accepted.

01 / DELIVERY

Shipping, made clear.

Delivery options, fees and estimated arrival windows depend on destination and fulfilment method. Any promise shown on this design preview is illustrative until the final policy is confirmed.

Before launch, confirm:

  • Countries and regions you ship to.
  • Shipping fees and the conditions for complimentary delivery.
  • Order processing times and estimated delivery windows.
  • Tracking details and what customers should do if a parcel is delayed.

02 / RETURNS

A straightforward return.

The final policy should explain the return period, item condition requirements, how to request a return, return postage responsibilities and refund timing. Make sure it follows the rules that apply to your business and customers.
