WHY OFFCUT EXISTS

Not more stuff.
Better stuff.

We don’t think every viral product deserves a place in your home. We’re building a small collection of useful finds selected for the way they work, not just the way they look on a screen.

OUR FILTER

Three things a product needs.

01

It has a purpose.

It should solve a small problem, improve a routine, or be genuinely enjoyable to use.

02

It holds up to scrutiny.

We check supplier details, materials, product reviews, delivery times, and whether the item is appropriate to sell.

03

The price makes sense.

Shipping, taxes, returns, and payment fees matter. A viral product is not automatically a good deal.
